Our Story

Deep Creek Plumbing is a family owned and operated plumbing business covering the Mid North Coast, from Taree and Wingham out to Forster, Tuncurry, Wauchope and Port Macquarie. We've been doing this for 15+ years across both residential and commercial jobs — blocked drains, hot water, gas fitting, renovations, and the emergency calls that can't wait.

We're not a call centre dispatching whoever's free. When you call Deep Creek Plumbing, you're talking to the people who'll actually turn up — and who'll still be around in town long after the job's done. We treat every job like it's our own home.
