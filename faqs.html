FAQs

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

The things people usually ask us before booking a job. Can't find yours? Just give us a call.

Do you offer emergency plumbing?

Yes. For burst pipes, flooding, gas smells or anything that can't wait, call our emergency line on 0432 863 654 any time, day or night.

What areas do you service?

We're based in Taree and cover the Mid North Coast — Taree, Wingham, Old Bar, Forster, Tuncurry, Wauchope, Port Macquarie and Harrington. Not on that list? Give us a call anyway, we're often happy to travel a little further.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, we're fully licensed for both general plumbing and gas fitting work, and fully insured.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes — quotes are fast, free and no-obligation. Tell us what's going on and we'll give you an honest idea of cost before any work starts.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday to Friday, 7am–5pm, and Saturday 8am–1pm. Outside those hours, our 24/7 emergency line is still open.

Do you handle commercial work?

Yes. We handle everything from a leaking tap at home to commercial maintenance, backflow prevention and compliance work.

What types of hot water systems do you service?

All of them — electric, gas, solar and heat pump systems. Repairs, servicing, and full replacements or new installs.

How quickly can you get to me?

For genuine emergencies, we aim to be on our way as soon as possible. For everything else, we'll usually have you booked in within a day or two — call us and we'll give you a straight answer.
