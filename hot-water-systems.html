Signs it's time to call

Catching these early usually means a repair, not a full replacement.

  • Hot water running out faster than it used to
  • Rumbling, popping or banging noises from the tank
  • Water that looks rusty or smells a bit off
  • Any pooling or dampness around the base of the unit
  • A system that's over 10 years old and never been serviced
