  1. 1
    With Holly

    Send an enquiry, or just ring

    However you reach out — the website form or a phone call — you'll be speaking with Holly directly. No call centre reading from a script, just someone local who already knows the area and will actually remember your job.

    “I read every message myself — if you're stressed about a leak, tell me and I'll call you straight back.— Holly

    Bryce comes out to have a look and quote on site. If that's not convenient, we can do the whole thing over a video call instead — whatever gets you an honest number the fastest.

    With Holly

    You get a detailed quote

    With Bryce

    Bryce turns up and gets the job finished properly, treating your home like his own — clean, tidy, and no shortcuts.

    “I treat every house like it's got someone I care about living in it, because around here, it usually is someone I know.— Bryce

    The follow-up

    We invoice and check in

    You'll get a straightforward invoice, and afterwards we check in to make sure everything's working the way it should and you're genuinely happy with the job — not just that it's done.

    “A job's not finished for me until I know you're actually happy — not just that the invoice went out.— Holly
