A Few Things Worth Knowing

Are you a licensed plumber?

Yes! Deep Creek Plumbing is fully licensed, with over 15 years of plumbing experience.

Where do you service?

We're a local plumbing business and service the surrounding local area.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes! We offer no-obligation free quotes for your plumbing needs.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No call-out fees. We believe in honest and upfront pricing.

Do you provide emergency plumbing?

Yes! We offer 24/7 emergency plumbing and after-hours services.

What type of plumbing work do you do?

We provide a wide range of residential and commercial plumbing services, including repairs, maintenance, hot water, gas, renovations and emergency plumbing.

Are you a family-owned business?

Yes! We're a husband and wife family business with children of our own. We understand how important your home is and treat your home like our own.

Every job is different. We provide good, competitive pricing and will explain the work and costs before proceeding.

Do you clean up after the job?

Absolutely. We take pride in providing clean, respectful and quality workmanship.

Why choose Deep Creek Plumbing?

We're local, honest, reliable and experienced, with over 15 years in the industry. As a family business, we genuinely care about our customers and the work we do.
