What to do while you wait

A few simple things that can save a lot of damage in the meantime.

1

Find your water meter

If it's a burst pipe or serious leak, turning off the tap at the meter stops it getting worse while you wait for us.

2

Move what you can

Get furniture, rugs and anything electrical away from the water. Towels down to soak up what's already there.

3

Call us

Tell us what's happening and we'll give you an honest idea of timing before we even leave the ute.
