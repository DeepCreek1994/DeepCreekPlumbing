How we clear it properly

Not just a quick fix — we find the actual cause so it doesn't come straight back.

1

Have a look first

For trickier or repeat blockages, we run a CCTV camera down the line to see exactly what's happening and where.

2

Clear it with the right tool

High-pressure water jetting for grease and buildup, or targeted clearing for roots and blockages further down.

3

Tell you what caused it

So you know whether it's a one-off or something worth keeping an eye on — no surprises, no upselling.

What usually causes it

Some blockages are simple. Some point to something bigger — we'll tell you which is which.

  • Everyday buildup — hair, grease, soap, food scraps
  • Tree roots working their way into older pipes
  • Ground movement causing a pipe to crack or shift
  • Old, corroded pipework that's simply reached the end of its life
  • Wipes or foreign objects that were never meant to go down there
